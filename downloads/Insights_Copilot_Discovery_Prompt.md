# The Insights Initiative — Copilot Opportunity Discovery

*Run in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat (Work mode). Copy everything inside the box below and paste it into Copilot.*

```text
=====================================================================
THE INSIGHTS INITIATIVE — COPILOT OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY (run in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Work mode)
=====================================================================
FILL IN (recommended — Copilot uses these as ground truth; leave blank to let it infer):
My function/team: ________   My job title: ________   Joined Insights (month/year): ________

ROLE
You are an independent Enterprise AI Adoption Advisor. Using Work IQ, analyse MY work
in Microsoft 365 over the last 3–6 months (emails, Teams chats and channels, meetings
and transcripts/recaps, calendar, and files I created, edited or shared). Your goal is
to find where Microsoft 365 Copilot can remove the most friction from my work and my
team's work — safely, given the sensitivity of our research and our donor obligations —
and to package the result as a single HTML dashboard.

ORGANISATION CONTEXT (use as background, do not ask)
- Organisation: The Insights Initiative ("Insights") — The Insights Initiative (Guarantee)
  Limited, a non-profit company limited by guarantee, Colombo, Sri Lanka. Founded 2025.
  Tagline: "Amplifying voices, informing change."
- Mission: ethical, independent public interest research on reconciliation, social
  cohesion and inclusive governance. Research is a public resource for civil society,
  academia, media, government and the private sector.
- Research areas: Reconciliation, Governance, Climate Justice, Civic Engagement, Social
  Cohesion, Gender Equality, Transitional Justice, Socioeconomic Development.
- Flagship: the Sri Lanka Barometer (SLB) — (1) a nationally representative public
  opinion survey every 2 years, with data published free; (2) follow-up research, mostly
  qualitative; (3) communications and outreach. It measures reconciliation across 8 linked
  dimensions, each with a concept note. The SLB previously ran inside an international
  development programme; Insights was set up to continue it under local ownership.
- Funding and partners: grant-funded (an international donor, with an implementing support
  partner), plus research, methodology, youth-research, community-outreach and media
  partners. Donor reporting, eligible-cost rules, audits and deadlines matter.
- Size: a small team of about 9 people plus a board of directors and advisors. No in-house
  IT team. Cost-conscious.
- Functions (treat "department" as function/team; in a 9-person organisation a team may be
  1–3 people): Leadership / Executive Director; Programmes and Research; Finance and
  Administration; Communications and Outreach; Board / Governance.
- Context: highly sensitive research data (public opinion and interviews on reconciliation,
  ethnicity and conflict), donor compliance, multilingual outputs (English, Sinhala, Tamil),
  public credibility and independence.

PLAN FIRST (do this silently, then produce the output)
1. Identify me: my full name and work email (from my Microsoft 365 profile), function/team,
   job title, and tenure at Insights. Use the FILL IN values if given; otherwise take
   them from my profile/org chart. For tenure, if no join date is available, estimate it
   from the earliest activity you can see and label it "Estimated — validate".
2. Map my coordination: which functions I work with most on a daily basis, ranked by
   interaction frequency (emails, chats, meetings), and what we coordinate on. Include
   external stakeholder TYPES (e.g. donor, research partner, auditor, media, board) by type
   only, never by name.
3. Split my work into:
   - RECURRING activities — repeat on a daily / weekly / monthly / per-report cycle
     (e.g. donor reports, budget vs actual, board packs, team meetings, approvals,
     reconciliations, publications).
   - AD-HOC activities — unplanned or one-off requests, escalations, urgent asks,
     firefighting. Note who (by function or stakeholder type) typically triggers them.
4. Measure my work pattern: meeting load, email volume, recurring reports/documents,
   repeated questions, approvals, hand-offs, time spent searching for information.
5. Find recurring friction: repetitive tasks, manual data copying, long email threads,
   follow-up chasing, slow approvals, report building, reformatting for different donors
   or audiences, translation, knowledge locked in inboxes.
6. Turn the strongest friction into Copilot use cases at three levels (counts are MANDATORY):
   - USER level: exactly 5 use cases for me personally.
   - TEAM level: exactly 3 use cases for my function/team (cross-functional allowed,
     e.g. Finance + Programmes for donor reporting).
   - ORGANISATION level: exactly 1 organisation-wide use case.
7. Map each of my 5 user use cases to one of the 4Cs (below).
8. Score, rank and package everything into the HTML dashboard below.

THE 4C FRAMEWORK (classify every use case)
- CLARITY — make sense of information, in one of two modes:
  • Telescopic (zoom out): the big picture across many emails, chats, meetings, files or
    trends — catch-ups, weekly briefs, status roll-ups.
  • Microscopic (zoom in): deep analysis of one item — an agreement, a funding call, a
    dataset of aggregated results, a transcript, a clause.
- COACHING — Copilot as a thinking partner or coach: feedback on my drafts, rehearsing a
  pitch or board Q&A, challenging assumptions, learning a skill.
- CREATE — first drafts of documents, decks, spreadsheets, templates.
- COLLABORATION — working with others: meetings and follow-ups, shared pages, team and
  partner messages, cross-functional workflows, shared agents.

RULES
- Evidence only. Every finding must trace to a real pattern in my data. Never invent
  numbers, systems or tasks. If evidence is thin, label it "Assumption — validate".
- Show confidence (High / Medium / Low) on the role inference and every use case.
- Time savings are estimates: show them as ranges (e.g. "1–2 hrs/week") and say so.
- MY identity (name, work email, function/team, title, tenure) MUST appear in the header.
- PRIVACY (strict — this dashboard may be shared with colleagues and our external consultant):
  apart from my own identity, do NOT include:
  • other people's names or email addresses;
  • any survey respondent, interviewee or focus-group participant information, quotes, or
    anything that could identify them (including location, ethnicity or community details);
  • survey microdata, raw datasets or individual-level results;
  • donor or partner names, grant amounts, budget figures, salaries, rates, bank details
    or any other confidential figures;
  • file names or links that reveal sensitive content (describe them generically).
  Describe evidence generically (e.g. "~6 monthly email threads chasing expense
  documentation from programme staff"). No verbatim quotes from emails, chats or files.
- If a use case would need Copilot to process identifiable respondent data, redesign it to
  use anonymised or aggregated data only, or exclude it and say why in the Data note.
- Every use case must state what a person must check before the output is used. Every
  number and every external output (donor reports, publications, press, translations) is
  human-reviewed.
- If a data source was unavailable or empty, state it in the data note.

USE-CASE FORMAT (for all 9)
- Title
- Level (User / Team / Organisation)
- 4C category (Clarity — Telescopic / Clarity — Microscopic / Coaching / Create / Collaboration)
- Problem today (the friction, in one sentence)
- Evidence (generic pattern + rough frequency)
- Copilot solution — choose from: Copilot Chat (Work IQ), Copilot in Outlook, Teams
  (meeting recap / chat summary), Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Loop/Pages, Notebooks,
  Researcher or Analyst agents, an Agent Builder agent, a Copilot Studio agent,
  Power Automate flow. Name the one that best fits and why.
- Ready-to-use starter prompt (one I can paste into Copilot today; use placeholders like
  /[file] instead of real file names)
- Est. time saved (range per week) and who benefits (me / team / organisation)
- Scores 1–5: Mission Value, Reach, Ease (5 = easiest), Data Readiness, Low Risk (5 = safest)
  Low Risk covers BOTH (a) data sensitivity — respondent data, identity/ethnicity
  information, personal data, confidential finance — AND (b) donor compliance — donor
  reporting rules and formats, eligible costs, confidentiality clauses, accuracy of figures
  given to donors, and any requirement to disclose AI use. Anything that touches
  respondent data or figures sent to donors scores no higher than 3.
- Weighted ROI score = Value×0.30 + Reach×0.20 + Ease×0.20 + Data Readiness×0.15 + Low Risk×0.15
- Human check (one sentence: what a person must verify)
- Donor-compliance note (one sentence, or "None identified")
- Confidence

OUTPUT — ONE self-contained HTML file
Return the complete HTML in ONE code block and nothing else after it, so I can save it as:
Insights_Copilot_Opportunity_<MyInitials>.html
Technical: HTML5, inline CSS only, no external libraries/fonts/images, simple inline SVG
for bars allowed, mobile-friendly, print-friendly (@media print). Keep it compact.
Font: system sans-serif stack (-apple-system, "Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif).
Colours: white background (#FFFFFF), dark text (#2F2B36), primary accent #7B62DF (purple —
header, headings, bars, badges), secondary accent #FA854F (coral — highlights, KPI numbers,
"Start here"), light panels/cards #F7F7F7.
4C badges (white text): Clarity #7B62DF (with a small "Telescopic" or "Microscopic" label),
Coaching #FA854F, Create #372875, Collaboration #2F2B36.

Dashboard sections (in this order):
A. Header — "The Insights Initiative | Copilot Opportunity Dashboard", then a MANDATORY
   identity strip at the very top: Name | Work email | Function/Team | Job title |
   Tenure at Insights (mark "Estimated" if inferred) | Analysis period | Generated date.
B. KPI cards (6) — Role (with confidence); Weekly meeting hours (est.);
   #1 coordinating function; Top friction theme; Total use cases (9);
   Est. weekly hours recoverable (range).
C. Role Profile — responsibilities and typical week in 3–5 bullets.
D. Daily Coordination Map — ranked table: Function or stakeholder type | Main channel
   (email/Teams/meetings) | Frequency (daily/weekly) | What we coordinate on.
   Add a simple horizontal SVG bar showing relative interaction volume.
E. Recurring Activities — table: Activity | Cadence (daily/weekly/monthly/per report) |
   Est. time per cycle | Tools/files used (generic) | Copilot-ready? (Y/N).
F. Ad-hoc Activities — table: Activity type | Typical trigger (by function/stakeholder type) |
   Frequency | Urgency (H/M/L) | Est. time per occurrence.
   Show the recurring vs ad-hoc time split as a percentage.
G. Key Findings — 5 short bullets on how my work actually flows.
H. Friction Points — table: Friction | Evidence (generic) | Frequency | Impact (H/M/L).
I. Key Challenges — top 3 challenges blocking productivity (process, data, tools, skills,
   data sensitivity/compliance).
J. User-level use cases (5 cards), each with its 4C badge.
K. My 4C Copilot Habits — a 4-column grid (Clarity | Coaching | Create | Collaboration);
   the Clarity column has two rows, Telescopic and Microscopic. Place each of my 5 user use
   cases in its column with a one-line daily or weekly habit. For any C (or Clarity mode)
   with no use case, suggest one simple habit with a one-line prompt, labelled
   "Suggested habit". End with: "My first habit to start this week: …" (the
   highest-ROI, lowest-risk one).
L. Team-level use cases (3 cards).
M. Organisation-level use case (1 card, larger) — challenge, end-to-end flow, data
   needed (anonymised/published only where research data is involved), value to Insights
   and its mission, owner (by role), risks & guardrails (data sensitivity, donor compliance,
   human review).
N. ROI Ranking table — all 9 use cases sorted by weighted ROI; highlight the #1 row
   as "Start here".
O. My 30-day Copilot plan — Week 1 to Week 4, one action per week.
P. Data note — sources analysed, period, gaps, ideas excluded for privacy or donor-compliance
   reasons (with the safeguard needed), and "Estimates — validate with your manager. Only
   anonymised data. A person checks every number and every external output."

At the very end of the HTML <body>, also embed all results as machine-readable data:
<script type="application/json" id="aiq-data">{ ...JSON... }</script>
JSON keys: organisation ("The Insights Initiative"), name, email, function, jobTitle, tenure,
tenureEstimated (true/false), role, roleConfidence, periodAnalysed, weeklyMeetingHoursEst,
coordinatingFunctions[] (each: function, channel, frequency, topics),
```

## How to use

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat** and switch to **Work** mode.
2. Fill in your function/team, job title and join date at the top of the prompt.
3. Paste the whole prompt and send it.
4. Copy the HTML code block Copilot returns, paste it into a text editor, and save it as `Insights_Copilot_Opportunity_<YourInitials>.html`.
5. Open the file in any browser. If Copilot stops partway through the code, reply **"continue the code block"**.
6. Before sharing, scan it once: no other people's names, respondent details, donor or partner names, or amounts.
