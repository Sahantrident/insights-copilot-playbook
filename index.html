🧠 AI basics

Three ideas before we build anything

Assistive vs agentic AI, the agent spectrum, and how to write a prompt that works first time.

🤝 Assistive AI vs 🤖 agentic AI

🤝 Assistive

You ask. It helps. You decide.

One step at a time, inside the app you're using. You stay in control of every step.

🙋 You prompt→✨ AI drafts→✅ You decide
📝 Word drafts a donor narrative📊 Excel flags variances📧 Outlook summarises a thread
🤖 Agentic

You set a goal. It plans and acts.

Several steps, using tools and knowledge, checking in with you at set points.

🎯 Goal→🗺️ Plan→⚙️ Act→🔍 Check→📤 Report
🔭 Researcher reads 40+ sources🔬 Analyst writes and runs Python📆 Month-End agent runs 5 checks

🧑‍⚖️ At Insights, both need a human in the loop. Agentic doesn't mean unsupervised.

🤖 What are agents?

They use AI to run a process alongside, or on behalf of, a person or team.
